## Water-cycle and hydrology monitoring from satellite data

1. NASA Earth Observatory: The Water Cycle  
   https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-water-cycle
2. NASA-JAXA Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM)  
   https://gpm.nasa.gov/GPM
3. NASA GPM Science Objectives  
   https://gpm.nasa.gov/missions/GPM/science-objectives
4. NASA Science: SMAP

    https://www.ou.edu/coe/labs/hydros

Usage in pages:

- Supported the overview of the field as a branch that tracks water across atmosphere, land, soil, surface water, snow/ice, and large-scale storage.
- Supported references to precipitation, soil moisture, total water storage, operational blended products, and new mission directions for inland water.
- Supported the lab examples as illustrations of how the subfield is practiced across satellite hydrology, hydrometeorology, and prediction workflows.
