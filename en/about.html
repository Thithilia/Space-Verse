About

Space-Verse is a community-led project that makes space-science learning more approachable for Vietnamese students.

We are building a practical starting point for learners who want to understand what to study, where to find solid materials, and how research areas connect to academic pathways.

Main audience

High-school students, undergraduates, and self-learners interested in astrophysics, Earth observation, remote sensing, and space technology.

Approach

We prioritize clear study structure, trustworthy links, and a bridge between introductory learning and more advanced topics.
