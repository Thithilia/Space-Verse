# Sources for Space-Verse research directions batch

Updated: 2026-05-23

This note records the authoritative sources used for the 2026-05-23 content pass on:

- `vi/fields/space-physics.html`
- `vi/fields/satellite-technology.html`
- `vi/fields/remote-sensing.html`

The goal of this batch was not to exhaust each field, but to replace skeletal overview pages with a stable starter structure: overview, subtopics, background, methods/data/tools, missions or datasets, learning path, and trustworthy source links.

5. ESA: The solar wind  
   https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/The_solar_wind
6. NOAA SWPC: About Space Weather  
   https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/about-space-weather
7. NOAA SWPC: Ionosphere  
   https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/index.php/phenomena/ionosphere

Usage in page:

- Framed the field around the Sun-Earth system, magnetosphere, ionosphere, and operational space-weather impacts.
- Supported references to open data, forecast operations, and the importance of solar wind and magnetospheric coupling.
