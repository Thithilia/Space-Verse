Usage in page:

- Supported the framing of gamma-ray astronomy as the study of the most energetic photon window and of extreme cosmic environments.
- Supported references to major source classes including pulsars, supernova remnants, black-hole systems, AGN/blazars, and gamma-ray bursts.
- Supported the distinction between space-based gamma observatories and ground-based Cherenkov facilities, plus examples of current and near-future missions.

Usage in page:

- Supported the explanation of microlensing as a gravitational brightening event rather than a direct image of the lensing object.

- Supported the emphasis on survey cadence, real-time alerts, follow-up, light-curve modeling, and Roman's future demographic survey role.
