Usage in pages:

- Supported the overview of carbon monitoring as a branch that infers sources, sinks, and variability from atmospheric greenhouse-gas observations rather than treating concentration maps as direct emissions maps.
- Supported references to OCO-2, OCO-3, GOSAT/GOSAT-2, ESA greenhouse-gas climate records, NOAA reference measurements, and CarbonTracker as representative missions and infrastructure.
- Supported the explanation that satellite greenhouse-gas work depends on validation and calibration against ground-based reference networks such as TCCON and NOAA's long-running measurement programs.
- Supported the research-group examples as illustrations of how the subfield is practiced across reference monitoring, community validation networks, remote sensing, and inverse modeling.

## Scope notes

- Content was kept introductory and avoided recent regional emissions claims, city rankings, or other volatile result-specific statements that would require frequent refresh.
