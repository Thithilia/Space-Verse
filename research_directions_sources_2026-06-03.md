2. ESA Climate Office: Permafrost CCI

3. ESA Climate Office: Tracking thawing ground: 27 Years of Permafrost data reveal critical climate change insights

Usage in pages:

- Supported the definition of permafrost, the role of the active layer, and the point that many satellite workflows infer subsurface conditions indirectly from surface temperature, snow, freeze-thaw, land cover, and deformation signals.
- Supported references to ESA Permafrost CCI as a long-term climate-data effort, HydroGNSS as an example of freeze-thaw monitoring, and ABoVE as an interdisciplinary Arctic-boreal program linking permafrost with hydrology, carbon, fire, and ecosystems.
- Supported the explanation that field networks such as GTN-P and PERMOS are part of the evidence chain needed to validate and interpret satellite-based permafrost products.
- Supported the research-practice examples around Alaska and mountain permafrost without treating one institution or network as the sole definition of the field.

## Scope notes

- Content was kept introductory and avoided specific recent methane-budget numbers, infrastructure-loss estimates, or local hazard statistics that would require tighter result-level sourcing.
- Lab and network pages were used to show how the field is practiced and monitored, not to generalize one group's priorities to the entire subfield.
