Leonard Susskind - The Theoretical Minimum

Classical mechanics:
- Newtonian mechanics: Euclidean coordinate system, Galilean transformation,
Newton's laws.
- Variational principle: generalized coordinates, action, principle of least action.
- Lagrangian mechanics: Euler-Lagrange equation, conservation laws, Noether's theorem.
- Hamiltonian mechanics: Poisson brackets, canonical transformations, Liouville's theorem.
- Topics: Kepler's problem, collisions, small oscillations.

Special relativity:

- Relativistic kinematics: Minkowski coordinates, postulates of special relativity, four-vectors,
Lorentz transformations, spacetime diagrams, invariants, relativistic velocity addition,
relativistic Doppler effect and the twin paradox.
- Relativistic dynamics: energy-momentum relation, four-momentum conservation, relativistic
force, particle interactions and decays.
- Relativistic electromagnetism: four-potential, Lorentz transformation of the field,
gauge invariance.

Advanced:
- Motion of rigid bodies.
- Three-body problem.
- Adiabatic invariants.
- Legendre transformation.
- Introduction to relativistic cosmology.
