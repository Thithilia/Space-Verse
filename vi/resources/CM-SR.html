Leonard Susskind - The Theoretical Minimum

Classical mechanics:
- Newtonian mechanics: Euclidean coordinate system, Galilean transformation, 
Newton's laws, 
- Variational principle: Generalized coordinates, Action, Principle of Least action.
- Lagrangian mechanics: Euler-Lagrange equation, conservation laws, Noether's theorem.
- Hamiltonian mechanics: Poisson bracket, canonical transformation, Liouville theorem.
- Topics: Kepler's problem, Collisions, Small oscillations.

Special Relativity: 

- Kinetics: Minkowski coordinate system, Postulates of Special Realtivity, 4-vector,
Lorentz transformation, space-time diagram, invariants, relative velocity, relativistic
Doppler effect, twin paradox.
- Kinematics: Einstein formula for energy-mass conversion, 4-momentum conservation, 
relativistic force, particle interaction and decay.
- Relativistic Electromagnetism: 4-potential, Lorentz transformation of the field,
gauge invariance.

Advanced:
- Motion of rigid body.
- Three body problem.
- Adiabatic invariants.
- Legendre transformation.
- Relativistic Cosmology.
