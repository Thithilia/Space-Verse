Cơ học lượng tử


Mandatory:
- Early quantum theory: the Bohr model, de Broglie wavelength, electron double-slit
experiment and Mach-Zehnder interferometer.
- Concepts of quantum mechanics: postulates, first quantization, the Copenhagen interpretation,
the uncertainty principle, wave functions and the Schrödinger equation.
- A particle in a one-dimensional potential: infinite and finite square wells, tunneling,
the harmonic oscillator and the free particle.
- Formulation of quantum mechanics: bra-ket notation, operators and commutators.
- A particle in a three-dimensional potential: angular momentum and the hydrogen atom.

Advanced:
- Discrete symmetries: parity and time reversal.
- Identical particles: Bose-Einstein and Fermi-Dirac statistics.
- Approximation methods: perturbation theory and the Born-Oppenheimer approximation.
- Atoms in electromagnetic fields: fine and hyperfine structure, the Zeeman effect and
spontaneous emission.

Very advanced:
- Quantum foundations: Entanglement, EPR paradox, Bell's inequality, Quantum
teleportation.
- Scattering theory.
