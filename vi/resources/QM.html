Trang chủ / Tài nguyên / QM 

Mandatory:
- Semi-quantum mechanics: Bohr's model, de Broglie wavelength, Electron double slits 
experiment, Mach-Zehder Interferometer.
- Concept of quantum mechanics: Postulates of quantum mechanics, first quantization,
Copenhagen interpretation, Uncertainty principle, wave functions, Schrodinger equations
- A particle in one dimensional potential: Infinite and finite square wells, tunneling 
effect, Harmonic Oscillator, Free particle.
- Formulation of quantum mechanics: Bra-Ket notation, Operator, Commutator.
- A particle in three dimensional potential: Angular momentum, Hydrogen model.

Advanced:
- Discrete symetries: Parity, Time reversal.
- Indentical particles: Bose-Einstein distribution, Fermi-Dirac distribution.
- Approximation methods: Perturbation theory, Born-Oppenheimer approximation.
- Atoms in Electromagnetic fields: Fine structure and hyperfine structure, Zeeman effect, 
spontaneous emissions.

Very advanced:
- Quantum foundations: Entanglement, EPR paradox, Bell's inequality, Quantum
teleportation.
- Scattering theory.
