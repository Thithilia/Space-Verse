Brief notes

Astronomy and Astrophysics

  • Solar systems, planetary orbit.
  • Solar physics, space physics.
  • Astrometry.
  • Planets.
  • Stars: stellar structure, star formation, stellar evolution.
  • Supernova.
  • Galaxies.
  • Compact objects: White dwarf, Neutron star, Black hole.
  • Exoplanets.
  • Interstellar medium, molecular cloud, radiative transfer.
  • Cosmic rays, neutrino, air showers, Cherenkov radiation.
  • Instrumentation: space-based and ground-based instruments, optical telescope, radio interferometer, calorimeter, gravitaional wave interferometer, high energy detectors.
  • Dark matter: WIMP.
  • Spectroscopy.
  • Magnetohydrodynamics.
  • Multi-messenger astronomy: Optical, Infrared, X-ray, gamma-ray, UV, radio, sub-milimeter, gravitational wave.
  • Astrochemistry.
  • Astrobiology.

Cosmology

  • Redshift, Hubble's law, Big Bang.
  • Dark energy, ΛCDM model.
  • Thermal history of the universe.
  • Inflationary universe.
  • Cosmic microwave background.
  • Gravitational waves.
