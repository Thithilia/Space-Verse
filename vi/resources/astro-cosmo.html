Brief notes:

Astronomy and Astrophysics:
- The Solar System and planetary orbits.
- Solar physics, space physics.
- Astrometry.
- Planets.
- Stars: stellar structure, star formation, stellar evolution.
- Supernovae.
- Galaxies.
- Compact objects: white dwarfs, neutron stars and black holes.
- Exoplanets.
- Interstellar medium, molecular clouds and radiative transfer.
- Astroparticle physics: cosmic rays, neutrinos, air showers and Cherenkov detectors.
- Instrumentation: space-based and ground-based instruments, optical telescope,
radio interferometer, calorimeter, gravitational-wave interferometer and high-energy
detectors.
- Dark matter: observational evidence and candidates such as WIMPs and axions.
- Spectroscopy.
- Magnetohydrodynamics.
- Multiwavelength astronomy: radio, submillimeter, infrared, optical, ultraviolet,
X-ray and gamma-ray observations.
- Multimessenger astronomy: photons, gravitational waves, neutrinos and cosmic rays.
- Astrochemistry.
- Astrobiology.

Cosmology:
- Redshift, Hubble's law, Big Bang.
- Dark energy and the ΛCDM model.
- Thermal history of the universe.
- Inflationary universe.
- Cosmic microwave background.
- Gravitational waves.
