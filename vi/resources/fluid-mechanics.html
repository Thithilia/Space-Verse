Cơ học chất lưu

  • Ideal fluid: Equation of continuity, Euler's viewpoint, Lagrange's viewpoint, Hydrostatics, Bernoulli's equation, the conservation of circulation, incompressible fluid, gravity waves.
  • Viscous fluid: viscosity, energy dissipation, Reynolds number, larminar flow, turbulent flow, oscillatory motion in a viscous fluid, damping of gravity waves, vorticity.
  • Turbulence: steady flow, rotary flow, instability of tangential discontinuities, jet.
  • Surface phenomena: capillary waves, Laplace's formula.
  • Sound: Sound waves, geometrical acoustics, propagation, Cherenkov radiation.
  • Shock waves.
  • Superfluid.
