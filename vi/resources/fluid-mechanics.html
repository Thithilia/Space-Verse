Cơ học chất lưu

- Ideal fluids: continuity equation, Eulerian and Lagrangian descriptions, hydrostatics,
Bernoulli's equation, conservation of circulation, incompressible flow and gravity waves.
- Viscous fluids: the Navier-Stokes equations, viscosity, energy dissipation, Reynolds number,
laminar and turbulent flow, oscillatory viscous flow, damping of gravity waves and vorticity.
- Turbulence: instabilities, energy cascades, vortical flows and jets.
- Surface phenomena: capillary waves, Laplace's formula.
- Sound: acoustic waves, geometrical acoustics, propagation, Mach cones and sonic booms.
- Shock waves.
- Superfluids.
