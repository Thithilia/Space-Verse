Preliminary

  • Linear Algebra.
  • Single variable Calculus.
  • Multiple variable Calculus
  • Probability and Statistics.
  • Vector Calculus.
  • Differential and Partial Differential equations.

Advanced

  • Group theory (HEP, AP).
  • Lie Algebra (HEP, GR)
  • Differential geometry (HEP, GR).
  • Complex Analysis (HEP, GR, A&A)
  • Fourier Analysis (A&A).
  • Discrete Mathematics (CS).

HEP = High Energy Physics, AP = Atomic Physics, GR = General Relativity,

A&A = Astronomy & Astrophsyics, CS = Computer Science
