Quang học

- Geometrical optics: light propagation, Snell's law, total internal reflection, plane mirrors,
curved mirrors and lenses.
- Wave optics: the Huygens-Fresnel principle, wave equation, interference, diffraction and polarization.
- Quantum optics: photons, the photoelectric effect, and spontaneous and stimulated emission.
- Photonics: lasers, optical fibers, photodetectors and integrated optical systems.
