Nhiệt động lực học và cơ học thống kê

Thermodynamics

  • The zeroth law: microstate, macrostate, thermodynamical system, ideal gas model, thermal equilibrium, equation of state, Quasi-Static Processes, heat capacity.
  • The first law: Internal energy, Heat, Work.
  • The second law: Entropy, Reversible Processes, Carnot Cycle, Clausius inequality.
  • The third law: Nernst's postulate.

Kinetic Theory of Gases

  • Mean Free Path, Diffusion, Viscosity, Thermal Conductivity, Maxwell distribution, The Boltzmann transport equation.
  • Stochastic processes: Brownian Motion, The Einstein relation, Fokker-Planck Equation.

Statistical Mechanics

  • Fundamentals: Microcanonical Ensemble, Temperature, Two-State Spin System, Canonical Ensemble, Chemical potential, Grand Canonical Ensemble, Entropy.
  • Classical gases: Partition function, ideal gas,
  • Quantum gases: Density of States, Ideal quantum gas, Debye model, Bose-Einstein condensation, Fermi gas, White dwarfs, Neutron stars.

Advanced

  • Black body radiation: Stefan-Boltzmann's law.
  • Thermodynamical functions, Maxwell's Relations.
  • Absorption & emission of radiation: Kirchoff's law.
  • Real gas models: Van der Waals model.
  • Phase transition: type I and type II, The Clausius-Clapeyron equation, Phase Equilibrium, Ising Chain

Very advanced

  • Navier-Stokes Equation
  • Crystal lattice.
