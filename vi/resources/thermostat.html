Nhiệt động lực học và cơ học thống kê


Thermodynamics:
- Foundations: thermodynamic systems, state variables, equations of state, quasi-static
processes, heat capacity and the ideal-gas model.
- The zeroth law: thermal equilibrium and the definition of temperature.
- The first law: internal energy, heat and work.
- The second law: entropy, reversible processes, the Carnot cycle and the Clausius inequality.
- The third law: Nernst's postulate.

Kinetic theory of gases:
- Mean free path, diffusion, viscosity, thermal conductivity, the Maxwell distribution
and the Boltzmann transport equation.
- Stochastic processes: Brownian motion, the Einstein relation and the Fokker-Planck equation.

Statistical mechanics:
- Fundamentals: microstates and macrostates, microcanonical ensemble, temperature, two-state
spin systems, canonical ensemble, chemical potential, grand canonical ensemble and entropy.
- Classical gases: partition functions and the ideal gas.
- Quantum gases: density of states, ideal quantum gases, the Debye model, Bose-Einstein
condensation and Fermi gases, with applications to white dwarfs and neutron stars.

Advanced:
- Blackbody radiation: the Stefan-Boltzmann law.
- Thermodynamic potentials and Maxwell relations.
- Absorption and emission of radiation: Kirchhoff's law.
- Real-gas models: the van der Waals model.
- Phase transitions: first-order and continuous transitions, the Clausius-Clapeyron equation,
phase equilibrium and the Ising model.

Further topics:
- Crystal lattices and statistical mechanics of solids.
